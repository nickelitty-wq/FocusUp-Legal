🎯 FocusUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2025

Simple Summary: We don't collect or store any of your personal data. Everything stays on your device. Period.

1. Information We Collect

None. FocusUp does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers. We have no servers, databases, or cloud storage containing your data.

2. Data Storage

All your productivity data is stored locally on your iOS device using Apple's secure storage:

  • Focus session history and statistics
  • Task lists and completion records
  • App preferences and settings
  • Premium purchase verification status

We have zero access to this data. It never leaves your device except through your own device backups (iCloud, iTunes).

3. Third-Party Services

FocusUp uses only Apple's services:

  • Apple App Store: For app distribution and updates
  • Apple StoreKit: For in-app purchase processing and verification

We do not use:

  • Analytics or tracking services
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integrations
  • Cloud storage services
  • Third-party databases

4. In-App Purchases

When you purchase FocusUp Premium:

  • Payment is processed entirely by Apple (we never see your payment information)
  • Apple provides us with only a verification token to confirm your purchase
  • Your purchase status is stored locally on your device
  • We verify purchases with Apple's servers only (no personal data is transmitted)
  • Your Apple ID and payment details remain with Apple

5. Network Activity

FocusUp makes network requests only for:

  • Purchase Verification: Contacting Apple's servers to verify your premium purchase status
  • App Updates: Checking for app updates through the App Store

No other network activity occurs. Your productivity data never leaves your device.

6. Email Feedback

If you use the "Send Feedback" feature in the app:

  • Your email is composed using your device's Mail app
  • You choose what information to include
  • The email may include device information (iOS version, device model) to help us debug issues
  • We only receive what you explicitly send via email
  • We use this information solely to respond to your feedback and improve the app

7. Device Backups

If you back up your device (iCloud or iTunes):

  • Your FocusUp data is included in your device backup
  • This is handled entirely by Apple
  • We have no access to your backups
  • Restoring from backup will restore your FocusUp data

8. Children's Privacy

FocusUp is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, there are no additional risks for children.

9. Your Rights & Data Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is viewable within the app
  • Delete: Delete the app to remove all data permanently
  • Export: Your data is backed up with your device (no separate export needed)
  • Portability: Data moves with your device backups

Since we don't collect your data, there's nothing for us to access, modify, or delete on our end.

10. Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • iOS device-level encryption and secure storage
  • Apple's security infrastructure for purchase verification
  • No transmission of personal data over the internet
  • No third-party access to your information

11. International Users

Since FocusUp stores all data locally on your device:

  • Your data stays in your country/region
  • No cross-border data transfers occur
  • GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations are inherently satisfied

12. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy:

  • The "Last updated" date at the top will change
  • We'll notify you through the app if changes are significant
  • Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of changes

13. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents: Since we don't collect any personal information, CCPA rights regarding data collection, sale, and deletion are not applicable. There is no data to request, delete, or opt out of selling.

14. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

EU/EEA residents: Since we don't process any personal data, GDPR obligations regarding data processing, storage, and transfer do not apply. There is no data controller or processor relationship.

  • Support: Visit our Support Page
Bottom Line: FocusUp respects your privacy completely. Your data is yours alone—stored on your device, never collected by us, never sold, never shared. We can't access your data even if we wanted to, because we don't have any servers or databases storing it.
