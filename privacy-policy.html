🎯 FocusUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2025

Simple Summary: We don't collect any of your personal data. Everything stays on your device. Period.

1. Information We Collect

None. FocusUp is designed with privacy first. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

2. Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using secure local storage:

  • Focus session history
  • Task lists
  • Productivity statistics
  • App preferences and settings
  • Premium purchase status

We have zero access to this data. It never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services

FocusUp uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store: For in-app purchases only
  • Expo Platform: For app distribution and updates

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

4. In-App Purchases

When you make a purchase:

  • Payment is processed by Apple/Google (we never see your payment info)
  • Your purchase receipt is stored locally on your device
  • We verify purchases through Apple/Google servers only

5. Notifications

If you enable notifications:

  • Push notification tokens are managed by Apple/Google
  • Notifications are sent locally from your device (no server)
  • You can disable notifications anytime in Settings

6. Children's Privacy

FocusUp is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View all your data in the app
  • Delete: Clear all data via Settings → Danger Zone
  • Export: Data stays on your device (use device backup)

8. Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • Device-level encryption (iOS/Android secure storage)
  • No network transmission
  • No cloud storage
  • No third-party access

9. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll notify you through the app and update the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us:

  • Email: nikhilitty77@gmail.com
Bottom Line: FocusUp respects your privacy. Your data is yours alone. We don't collect it, sell it, or share it. Ever.
