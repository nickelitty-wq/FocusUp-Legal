2. Description of Service

FocusUp is a premium productivity application that provides:

  • Unlimited focus sessions with 5 focus modes (Pomodoro technique)
  • Task management and organization
  • AI-powered focus plan generation (optional feature, requires internet connection)
  • Session quality tracking and statistics
  • App blocking intent tracking (reminder system)
  • Premium themes and customization
  • Local data storage (all data stored on your device)
Important Note on App Blocking:

The app blocking feature tracks your intended blocks and provides anti-distraction reminders when you try to open blocked apps during focus sessions. However, it does not enforce system-level app blocking that would prevent you from opening apps entirely.

Full system-level app blocking would require integration with iOS Screen Time API or Android Digital Wellbeing, which needs a custom native build with special permissions and is not available in the standard version. The current version helps you track which apps you want to avoid during focus sessions and reminds you of your commitment when you attempt to use them.
