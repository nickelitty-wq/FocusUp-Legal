3. Distraction Prevention Feature

Important Clarification: FocusUp's distraction prevention feature uses psychological friction and intent-based blocking, not system-level app blocking.

How it works:

  • You select apps you want to avoid during focus sessions
  • The app tracks your distraction prevention goals
  • When enabled, you'll encounter friction (wait timers, challenges, warnings) before disabling blocks
  • In "Hardcore Mode," additional barriers help you stay focused

What it does NOT do:

  • Does not prevent you from opening other apps at the system level
  • Does not enforce blocks if you force-quit FocusUp
  • Does not require device administrator permissions
  • Relies on your commitment and self-discipline

FocusUp is designed to help you build better habits through awareness and psychological barriers, not technical enforcement.
