📋 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app → Go to Settings → Tap "Restore Purchases". Your premium features will be restored automatically if you've purchased them on this Apple ID.

Can I use FocusUp on multiple devices?

Yes! Your premium purchase is tied to your Apple ID. Simply download FocusUp on any device using the same Apple ID and tap "Restore Purchases" to unlock premium features.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → FocusUp → Cancel Subscription. You'll retain access until the current billing period ends.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All your data stays on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Check our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are processed through Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find your FocusUp purchase, and request a refund. Apple typically responds within 48 hours.

The app isn't working properly. What should I do?

Try these steps: 1) Force close and reopen the app, 2) Restart your device, 3) Check for app updates in the App Store, 4) If issues persist, email us at nikhilitty77@gmail.com with details about the problem.

Can I suggest a new feature?

Yes! We love hearing from users. Use the "Send Feedback" button in the app or email us directly. We read every suggestion and many features come from user requests.

Is there a free trial?

FocusUp requires a one-time purchase or subscription to unlock all features. We believe in providing value upfront with a complete onboarding experience so you know exactly what you're getting.
