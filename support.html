How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone:

  1. Open Settings app
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap "Subscriptions"
  4. Find FocusUp and tap "Cancel Subscription"

On Android:

  1. Open Google Play Store
  2. Tap Menu → Subscriptions
  3. Find FocusUp and tap "Cancel Subscription"

Or visit: Apple's Subscription Management

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are processed by Apple or Google, not by us:

  • iOS: Visit reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Android: Visit Google Play refund request
  • Request refund within 90 days of purchase
  • Approval is at Apple/Google's discretion

Can I restore my purchase on another device?

Yes! Tap "Restore Purchases" on the paywall screen. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used for the original purchase.

✨ Features & Usage

What is the AI Focus Plan feature?

The AI feature analyzes your tasks and generates personalized focus plans using Rork AI Toolkit. When you tap "Generate AI Plan," your task descriptions are sent to the AI service to create a customized productivity strategy. This feature requires an internet connection.

Is my data synced across devices?

No. All your data is stored locally on your device only. There is no cloud sync between devices. However, your data is included in your device's iCloud or iTunes backup if you have backups enabled.

Does the app actually block other apps?

The current version tracks which apps you want to block during focus sessions and provides anti-distraction reminders, but it does not enforce system-level app blocking. Full app blocking would require integration with iOS Screen Time API or Android Digital Wellbeing, which needs a custom native build and special permissions.

What data does FocusUp collect?

FocusUp stores all your productivity data (tasks, focus sessions, settings) locally on your device. When you use the AI plan feature, only your task descriptions are sent to Rork AI to generate focus plans - no personal information is collected or transmitted. See our Privacy Policy for complete details.

🔧 Technical Issues

The app crashed or won't open

  1. Force quit the app (swipe up from app switcher on iOS, or use recent apps on Android)
  2. Restart your device
  3. Check for app updates in the App Store or Play Store
  4. If the problem persists, email support with your device model and OS version

My purchase isn't working

  1. Check your internet connection
  2. Tap "Restore Purchases" on the paywall screen
  3. Verify the purchase went through in App Store → Account → Purchase History (iOS) or Play Store → Account → Payments & subscriptions (Android)
  4. Still having issues? Contact support with your order receipt

The AI plan feature isn't working

  1. Make sure you have an active internet connection
  2. Check if you have tasks added (AI needs tasks to generate plans)
  3. Try closing and reopening the app
  4. If it still doesn't work, contact support

🔒 Data & Privacy

How do I delete my data?

Delete the FocusUp app from your device to permanently remove all locally stored data. If you used the AI plan feature and want to request deletion of data sent to Rork AI, please contact support at nikhilitty77@gmail.com.

Can I export my data?

Your data is automatically included in your device's iCloud or iTunes backups. There is currently no manual export feature, but this may be added in future updates.

Who can see my tasks and focus sessions?

Only you. All your data is stored locally on your device. We cannot access your data. The only exception is when you use the AI plan feature - in that case, only your task descriptions (not your personal information) are sent to Rork AI to generate focus plans.
