Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does the distraction prevention feature work?

A: FocusUp uses psychological friction to help you resist distractions. When you enable blocks for certain apps and try to disable them during a focus session, you'll encounter barriers like wait timers, simple challenges, and streak warnings. This gives you time to reconsider and stay focused. Note: This is not system-level blocking—it relies on your commitment.

Q: What is Hardcore Mode?

A: Hardcore Mode adds maximum friction when you try to disable app blocks during an active focus session. It includes a 60-second countdown timer, a simple math problem, and warnings about losing your streak. Enable it in Settings for serious focus commitment.

Q: Is my data stored in the cloud?

A: No. All your personal data (tasks, focus sessions, preferences, settings) is stored locally on your device only. We do not sync your data to the cloud or store it on external servers. Your data is private and stays on your device.

Q: How does the AI plan generation work?

A: When you tap "Generate AI Plan," FocusUp analyzes your current task list using a local algorithm on your device. It generates personalized focus recommendations with time estimates based on your tasks and productivity patterns. All processing happens on your device—no data is sent to external servers or stored elsewhere.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?

A: Subscriptions are managed through your App Store (iOS) or Play Store (Android) account. To cancel:

  • iOS: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → FocusUp → Cancel Subscription
  • Android: Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions → FocusUp → Cancel Subscription

Q: Can I restore my purchase on a new device?

A: Yes! Tap "Restore Purchases" on the paywall screen. Your subscription status will be restored using your App Store or Play Store account.

Q: What subscription options are available?

A: FocusUp offers flexible subscription options to fit your needs:

  • Weekly: Perfect for trying out premium features
  • Monthly: Flexible month-to-month access
  • Annual: Best value with significant savings
  • Lifetime: One-time purchase for unlimited access
Pricing is displayed in your local currency in the app. All subscriptions include full access to premium features.

Q: How do streaks work?

A: Your streak increases by 1 each day you complete at least one focus session. Maintain your streak to build consistency and unlock higher focus scores!

Q: What is the Focus Score?

A: Your Focus Score (0-100) measures your productivity based on:

  • Session completion rate (finishing sessions you start)
  • Quality ratings (how well you focused during sessions)
  • Temptation resistance (staying in the app during focus sessions)
Higher scores indicate better focus habits!

Q: How do I request a refund?

A: Refund requests must be made through your App Store or Play Store account, as we don't process payments directly. Refunds are subject to Apple's and Google's refund policies.
